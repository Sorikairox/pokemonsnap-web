### Game C / rules (verify first — may be correct behaviour)
1. **Volcano: cart blocked by the Moltres egg when the player does nothing** (`c2n`: course_route 2 `--no-play`,
   retraces 5700..10500 the view is the egg). Mechanism is the game's own: `BumpDetector_Update` (app_level/
   bump_detect.c) -> `processBump` (player.c) stops the cart while a tangible Pokémon with `POKEMON_FLAG_20` is within
   5 units; the egg has FLAG_20 and idles forever unless hit. Likely console behaviour (like the River's Porygon),
   but check the egg's spawn position / `collisionRadius` against an emulator: if the console's cart passes the egg,
   this is a port bug in pokemon placement (ground height, spawn data relocation). Repro: `node port/web/run.mjs
   --out port_work/X --save all6.fla $(node port/web/course_route.mjs 2 --frames 10500 --no-play)`.
